## What's Changed
* Use the correct ENTSO-E codes for Italian bidding zones by @enoch85 in https://github.com/enoch85/ge-spot/pull/114
* Fix price availability and reduce routine log noise by @enoch85 in https://github.com/enoch85/ge-spot/pull/113

### Key Changes

- **Timely cached prices**: Sensors update at each price interval while provider requests are pending, with updates spread across areas to reduce simultaneous load.
- **Reliable midnight rollover**: Cached forecasts move to the correct display day before a refresh finishes, including delayed callbacks and restarts. Fresh prices for today take precedence over yesterday's forecast.
- **Provider recovery**: Optional forecasts no longer hold up available prices. Transient server and connection failures receive a bounded retry, and failed sources become eligible again after their cooldown.
- **Correct delivery intervals**: Improve market-day selection, interval alignment and delivery-period handling for Nord Pool, Energi Data Service, AEMO, ComEd and ENTSO-E.
- **Italian bidding zones**: Correct the ENTSO-E area codes, add Centre-North and Calabria coverage, and discard persisted ENTSO-E prices fetched with an outdated area code.
- **Quieter routine logs**: Handled provider conditions use DEBUG, sustained missing prices warn once, and authentication and unexpected errors remain visible.
